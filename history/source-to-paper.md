# Where the BSD research appears

This guide connects the recovered research record to the public working papers. A file may contain a proof, a correction to an earlier claim, or a computation whose original inputs have since been lost. The papers state what can be proved from the inputs that could be checked. Neither the general rank conjecture nor the full leading-coefficient formula is established by this collection.

## What an independent reader can verify

The original calculations reported first or second moments with final residues 54 modulo 109 and 60 modulo 389. The two original auxiliary-level graphs, their eigenvectors and the complete labeled CM data were not recovered together. An available 196-state Brandt matrix at auxiliary prime 7 belongs to another calculation. The numeric claims therefore carry no standalone reproducibility claim here. The finite-moment paper preserves the general criteria and the rank and group conclusions conditional on their inputs.

Other explicitly open inputs include a canonical nonzero derived class at the chosen three-prime support; a uniform signed comparison after continuing the full height/Fourier expression; the construction of the original finite GU character; and a simultaneous Frobenius prescription across all overlapping twisted Selmer spaces for the strongest multiquadratic assertion. The recovered individual witness theorems do not prove that simultaneous assertion.

These are public working papers, checked internally against the recovered proofs and cited literature. Neither mathematical priority nor independent specialist certification follows from the source mapping. The public historical archive is a research snapshot; the full November 17 compilation, including binary payloads and original aliases, is not in that ZIP.
