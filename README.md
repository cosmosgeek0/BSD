## Research selection

**Selected for specialist review.** For the eligible negative prime twists of $y^2=x^3-28x+1$, the sign at a single quartic root determines when the Cassels–Tate pairing is nondegenerate. The negative sign forces rational rank zero and a 2-primary Tate–Shafarevich group of type (2,2). The revised note also proves that a positive-density subset of these primes has nonzero complex central value. The case-specific steps are a parity relation for real modular symbols, a certified value at 31, and simultaneous control of the analytic coefficient and pairing sign. The general BSD rank equality and the priority of this specialization remain open for review. [Selected note (PDF)](fisher-twist-rank-zero.pdf) · [LaTeX](fisher-twist-rank-zero.tex) · [Authoring chat](https://chatgpt.com/c/6b01184f-5988-83e8-9d96-c42162fe07c2) · [Independent seed check](https://chatgpt.com/c/6af068e6-a3b4-8320-b137-01d03fe069a1) · [Sirolli–Tornaría](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.04483) · [Fisher](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40993-022-00376-z)

**Fourier residues and rational chords.** For a plane cubic representing any elliptic curve over the rationals, a prime-power Fourier sum selects the rational chords, with exact weights for tangents and flexes. Counting the weighted directions recovers the Mordell–Weil rank. Taking the multiples of one direction recovers the order of the original elliptic L-function by analytic continuation. An appendix combines the real integral with the factors at every prime on a pencil through a nonflex rational point. The resulting boundary coefficient grows with exponent half the rational rank and has no meromorphic extension at zero in odd rank. A second appendix identifies the positive squared Fourier distribution on tangent directions at every finite precision and quantifies the loss from treating rational reductions at different primes as independent. Appendix C proves a compensated lognormal fluctuation law in the independent-prime model, including the variance log 2. The equality of the two rank measurements and the priority of the specialization remain open. Appendices D and E separate the rational cone and nonsolution divisor sum and compute the correction introduced by a polar character. The final two appendices give uniform rational-pencil geometry and the finite prime-square tangent calculation on the cubic. [Working manuscript (PDF)](plane-cubic-fourier-residues.pdf) · [LaTeX](plane-cubic-fourier-residues.tex) · [Original account chat](https://chatgpt.com/c/6b01184f-5988-83e8-9d96-c42162fe07c2) · [Local Fourier precedent](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9904008)

**Splitting genus-one torsors and rank growth.** Let $E$ be an elliptic curve over a totally real number field $K$, with irreducible $E[2]$. If a locally soluble torsor has exact period $2^k$ with $k\geq 1$, there is a tower of $k$ totally real quadratic extensions that splits it, with prescribed splitting at any finite set of finite places. The rank increases at every step. In the normal closure $L$, the bound is $\operatorname{rank} E(L)-\operatorname{rank} E(K)\geq 2^k-1$. This result concerns rank after base extension. The height estimates bound the transverse component of a Kummer representative and the number of small representatives across linearly disjoint fields. The integrated Picard-group calculation gives a criterion for lifting Frobenius graph correspondences between two torsors. ## Further working papers

- **Central-derivative quadratures for elliptic modular forms.** The paper derives squarefree modular-symbol and Kummer-correlated formulas with explicit correction terms and gives a same-curve control for the bounded integer quadrature. [PDF](central-derivative-quadratures.pdf) · [LaTeX](central-derivative-quadratures.tex)

- **Integral splittings in split and unitary rank-three compact models.** The paper derives the integral projector and its denominator in the split model, and the ten-orbit boundary and constant splitting in the inert model. [PDF](compact-section-splittings.pdf) · [LaTeX](compact-section-splittings.tex)

- **Cyclic splitting fields, integral norms and Heegner derivatives.** The paper bounds the number of cyclic fields that can split a torsor under a rank-growth hypothesis, calculates the one-orbit integral norm index and gives conditional equality of relative ranks for a primitive Heegner derivative. [PDF](cyclic-splitting-fields.pdf) · [LaTeX](cyclic-splitting-fields.tex)

- **Splitting densities of Selmer and Mordell–Weil classes.** Field indices and simultaneous prime-splitting densities recover the orders of finite-level Tate–Shafarevich subgroups under the stated image hypotheses. [PDF](selmer-splitting-densities.pdf) · [LaTeX](selmer-splitting-densities.tex)

- **Genus characters in a fixed imaginary quadratic field.** The construction detects independent marked points modulo two using one fixed rank-one twist, under the stated sign and irreducibility hypotheses. [PDF](fixed-field-genus-characters.pdf) · [LaTeX](fixed-field-genus-characters.tex)

- **Dihedral branch cycles.** Explicit covers, their branch cycles and arithmetic points recovered from the original geometry record. [PDF](dihedral-branch-cycles.pdf) · [LaTeX](dihedral-branch-cycles.tex)

- **Finite coefficient lifts and integral jets of cyclotomic Gauss rows.** A length inequality under explicit module and cocycle assumptions, sharp counterexamples, and finite-precision Gauss-row calculations. Existence of the required arithmetic lift remains open. The combined edition includes the all-character averaging calculation, ramification correction and a semistable abelian-surface example with unbounded projected monodromy index. [PDF](finite-precision-descent.pdf) · [LaTeX](finite-precision-descent.tex)

- **Modular-unit fibre heights and CM translates of central Mellin integrals.** Modular units control height pairings on fibres, while CM translates on a non-CM curve test the arithmetic content of central Mellin integrals. [PDF](modular-fibre-heights.pdf) · [LaTeX](modular-fibre-heights.tex)

- **Analytic rank in quadratic twist families with two sections.** Two independent sections and prescribed local square conditions give twists of analytic rank at least three. The paper separates unconditional counts from upper bounds that assume the Riemann hypothesis for the twists. An unconditional almost-prime subfamily has a fixed upper bound on its algebraic rank. [PDF](two-section-twist-families.pdf) · [LaTeX](two-section-twist-families.tex)

- **A determinant identity for fixed-level Hecke operators.** Under the stated local hypotheses, a chain of Hecke operators detects independent mod-two Selmer directions. The paper gives a bound on lifting the identity in the congruence lattice. [PDF](fixed-hecke-chain.pdf) · [LaTeX](fixed-hecke-chain.tex)

- **A conditional three-prime Kolyvagin-class test for 234446.a1.** Three primes single out a line in the rational mod-seven Kummer space. The paper gives a finite-field detector at 19. The identification of the derived class uses stated Selmer hypotheses, and its scalar remains unevaluated. [PDF](three-prime-localization.pdf) · [LaTeX](three-prime-localization.tex)
- **A Kurihara witness and the leading values of all full-support twists.** A nonzero modular-symbol witness determines the cyclotomic valuation of the normalized central values of the specified twists. The Selmer and Tate–Shafarevich conclusions have additional hypotheses. [PDF](kurihara-full-support-twists.pdf) · [LaTeX](kurihara-full-support-twists.tex)

- **Quartic halving fields and rank growth.** Explicit trace-zero points in a quartic halving field and rank growth on rational specializations. [PDF](quartic-halving-rank-growth.pdf) · [LaTeX](quartic-halving-rank-growth.tex)

- **Effective detection by reduction at finitely many primes.** Under a full Kummer-image hypothesis, a subgroup of rank r modulo a prime power is detected by r distinct good primes with an explicit size bound. [PDF](effective-kummer-detection.pdf) · [LaTeX](effective-kummer-detection.tex)

- **Restriction and doubling over a quadratic extension.** Matching local two-Selmer structures and two nondegenerate pairings give a rank-stable restriction formula for the two-primary Tate–Shafarevich group. For full S₃ action on two-torsion and even two-Selmer dimension at least four, the revised proof constructs an infinite rank-stable quadratic tower with prescribed local splitting. [PDF](quadratic-restriction-doubling.pdf) · [LaTeX](quadratic-restriction-doubling.tex)

- **Anticyclotomic traces of the Tate theta function.** A local norm-compatible construction gives a nonzero limiting trace congruent to 162 modulo 243. [PDF](anticyclotomic-theta-traces.pdf) · [LaTeX](anticyclotomic-theta-traces.tex)

- **Descent indices from prime sums and marked reductions.** A continued prime sum measures multiquadratic descent indices, with applications to two-isogeny descent and marked reductions. The final sections treat fixed-prime Heegner-height moments, split-prime sampling, covector orbit fields, and limits for local and global character weights. The final version treats both real and imaginary quadratic sampling and proves the orbit formula at composite modulus. [PDF](marked-prime-reductions.pdf) · [LaTeX](marked-prime-reductions.tex)

- **Moving rank and the order of a first cohomology class.** For finite abelian p-groups, an order-p⁴ class requires moving rank at least p⁴−1. Includes the sharp example and comparison with the cyclic case. The extension treats the cyclic exterior norm, simultaneous elementary divisors and the mixed two-prime cohomology calculation. [PDF](cohomological-rank-bound.pdf) · [LaTeX](cohomological-rank-bound.tex)

- **A quartic governing character for a twist family.** An explicit quadratic character on a quartic field identifies the pairing sign and proves equidistribution under the stated local conditions. The three explicit quartics are identified as halving fields of sums of rational points, with degree certificates. [PDF](quartic-governing-character.pdf) · [LaTeX](quartic-governing-character.tex)

- **Prescribed quadratic traces on elliptic curves.** A genus-two construction realizes two prescribed traces under cyclic-isogeny hypotheses, with branch-degree, height and exceptional-set estimates. The 73, −359 and −911 specializations include exact traces and a finite-field certificate for three independent points. [PDF](prescribed-quadratic-traces.pdf) · [LaTeX](prescribed-quadratic-traces.tex)

- **Translated periods and integral Hecke congruences at conductor 234446.** Positive translated periods coexist with a changing-sign Laplace density. The three-eigenform integral Hecke lattice has index four. [PDF](translated-periods-234446.pdf) · [LaTeX](translated-periods-234446.tex)
- **Marked elliptic covers.** The paper combines a genus-two marking, its Kummer parity, a nodal Selmer comparison and a sharp visibility dimension under large residual image. The visibility bounds include sharp minimal marking ranks for a nontorsion point and a prescribed period-p torsor. [PDF](marked-elliptic-covers.pdf) · [LaTeX](marked-elliptic-covers.tex)

- **Ranks of two elliptic K3 surfaces obtained by quadratic base change.** Geometric rank four, rank three over the rational function field with rational constants, and infinitely many specializations with four independent points. [PDF](quadratic-base-change-k3.pdf) · [LaTeX](quadratic-base-change-k3.tex)

- **Matrix coset averages in the boundary model of the Steinberg representation.** Exact finite-level averages, dependence on Smith invariants, and an all-conductor compact inert integral for the specified test function. [PDF](steinberg-coset-averages.pdf) · [LaTeX](steinberg-coset-averages.tex)

- **Real origin fibres of modular parametrizations.** The note relates the central order to interior real crossings and gives degree bounds with a separate CM refinement. The paper includes flat-character moments, the completed spin value for the second derivative and the rescaled Artin comparison. A CM comparison describes the circular mean and the multiplicity in the elliptic variable. The Artin comparison includes the construction of its auxiliary field. [PDF](real-origin-fibres.pdf) · [LaTeX](real-origin-fibres.tex)
- **A certified four-zero disk for 234446.a1.** The interval proof counts four zeros with multiplicity in a disk of radius $10^{-57}$ about the centre. The individual central multiplicity remains two or four. [PDF](central-zero-disk.pdf) · [LaTeX](central-zero-disk.tex)
